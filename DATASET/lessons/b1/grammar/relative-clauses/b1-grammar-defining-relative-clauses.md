# Resumen

Una defining relative clause identifica de qué persona, cosa o lugar hablamos.
Sin ella, la frase pierde su sentido.

`The woman who lives next door is a doctor.`  
`The book that I borrowed is on the table.`

# Objetivos

- Usar `who` para personas y `which`/`that` para cosas.
- Usar `where` para lugares.
- Reconocer cuándo el pronombre relativo es objeto y puede omitirse.
- Distinguir defining de non-defining.

# Explicación

## Pronombres relativos

`who` para personas:

`The man who called is my uncle.`

`which` o `that` para cosas:

`The film which we saw was great.`  
`The film that we saw was great.`

`where` para lugares:

`This is the café where we met.`

## Sujeto u objeto

Cuando el pronombre es el sujeto, no se omite:

`The person who called left a message.`

Cuando el pronombre es el objeto, puede omitirse:

`The book (that) I read was interesting.`

## Sin comas

Las defining clauses no llevan comas porque son esenciales.

# Forma o estructura

- Persona: `who` / `that`
- Cosa: `which` / `that`
- Lugar: `where`
- Objeto omitible: `(that/which/who)`

# Usos principales

1. **Identificar a una persona:** `The teacher who taught me is retired.`
2. **Identificar una cosa:** `The phone that I bought is cheap.`
3. **Identificar un lugar:** `This is the town where I grew up.`
4. **Añadir información esencial:** `The keys that are on the desk are mine.`

# Contrastes importantes

## who frente a which

`who` para personas; `which` para cosas.

`The doctor who treated me was kind.`  
`The hospital which opened last year is modern.`

## Definir frente a añadir

Defining: `The car that is red is mine.` (esencial)  
Non-defining: `My car, which is red, is parked outside.` (no esencial, con comas)

# Ejemplos

1. **The woman who works at the bank is my neighbour.**
2. **The restaurant that we visited was excellent.**
3. **This is the park where the children play.**
4. **The laptop which I use is quite old.**
5. **The person who answered the phone was helpful.**
6. **The book that you recommended is on sale.**
7. **That's the shop where I bought these shoes.**
8. **The train which leaves at six is the fastest.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ *The man which called is my uncle.*  
  ✅ *The man who called is my uncle.*  
  Para personas usamos `who`.

- ❌ *This is the place where we met at.*  
  ✅ *This is the place where we met.*  
  No añadimos `at` después de `where`.

- ❌ *The book who I read was good.*  
  ✅ *The book that I read was good.*  
  Para cosas usamos `that`/`which`.

- ❌ *The woman, who lives here, is kind.* (si es esencial)  
  ✅ *The woman who lives here is kind.*  
  Las defining clauses no llevan comas.

# Excepciones relevantes

`that` puede usarse para personas en lugar de `who` en registro informal:

`The man that called is my uncle.`

`whose` expresa posesión:

`The woman whose car was stolen called the police.`

Cuando el pronombre es objeto, omitirlo es natural en inglés hablado:

`The film (that) we saw was great.`

# Mini resumen

- `who` persona; `which`/`that` cosa; `where` lugar.
- Defining = esencial, sin comas.
- Objeto relativo puede omitirse.

# Comprobación rápida autocorregible

1. The woman ___ lives next door is a doctor. (persona)
   - Respuesta: `who`
   - Explicación: para personas usamos `who`.
2. The book ___ I borrowed is on the table. (cosa)
   - Respuesta: `that` (o `which`)
   - Explicación: para cosas usamos `that`/`which`.
3. This is the café ___ we met. (lugar)
   - Respuesta: `where`
   - Explicación: para lugares usamos `where`.
4. The man ___ called is my uncle. (sujeto, no se omite)
   - Respuesta: `who`
   - Explicación: como sujeto, no se omite.
5. The film ___ we saw was great. (objeto, omitible)
   - Respuesta: `that` (o `which`, o nada)
   - Explicación: como objeto, puede omitirse.
