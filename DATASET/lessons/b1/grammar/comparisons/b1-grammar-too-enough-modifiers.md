# Resumen

`too` indica un exceso que impide algo; `enough` indica la cantidad suficiente.

`The coffee is too hot to drink.`  
`The coffee is hot enough to drink.`

También podemos precisar cuánto difieren dos cosas:

`This flat is much bigger than the studio.`

# Objetivos

- Colocar `too` delante del adjetivo.
- Colocar `enough` después del adjetivo.
- Usar `enough` delante de sustantivos.
- Modificar comparativos con `much`, `a lot`, `a bit` y `far`.
- Distinguir el matiz negativo de `too` del neutro de `enough`.

# Explicación

## Too + adjetivo

`too` significa "más de lo necesario" y suele tener un matiz negativo:

`The suitcase is too heavy to carry.`

## Adjetivo + enough

`enough` va después del adjetivo:

`The room is warm enough.`

## Enough + sustantivo

Con sustantivos, `enough` va delante:

`We have enough chairs for everyone.`

## Modificadores de comparativo

Podemos precisar la diferencia:

`much` y `a lot` indican una gran diferencia:

`This model is much faster than the old one.`

`a bit` y `slightly` indican una pequeña diferencia:

`This route is a bit longer than the other one.`

`far` también intensifica:

`The new office is far more spacious than the old one.`

# Forma o estructura

- `too + adjetivo`
- `adjetivo + enough`
- `enough + sustantivo`
- `much / a lot / far + comparativo`
- `a bit / slightly + comparativo`

# Usos principales

1. **Exceso que impide:** `The box is too heavy to lift.`
2. **Cantidad suficiente:** `We have enough time to finish.`
3. **Gran diferencia:** `This phone is much cheaper than that one.`
4. **Pequeña diferencia:** `The new plan is a bit better than the old one.`
5. **Capacidad:** `She is old enough to drive.`

# Contrastes importantes

## too frente a enough

`too` es negativo: `The soup is too salty.`  
`enough` es suficiente: `The soup is salty enough.`

## Posición de enough

Con adjetivo va después; con sustantivo va delante:

`The water is warm enough.`  
`We have enough water.`

## much frente a a bit

`much` = gran diferencia; `a bit` = pequeña diferencia.

# Ejemplos

1. **The bag is too small for all my books.**
2. **The room is big enough for two desks.**
3. **We don't have enough time to visit the museum.**
4. **This laptop is much faster than my old one.**
5. **The new route is a bit shorter than the usual one.**
6. **The coffee is too strong for me.**
7. **There are enough seats for everyone.**
8. **The flat is far more expensive than we expected.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ *The coffee is enough hot.*  
  ✅ *The coffee is hot enough.*  
  Con adjetivo, `enough` va después.

- ❌ *We have time enough.*  
  ✅ *We have enough time.*  
  Con sustantivo, `enough` va delante.

- ❌ *The box is too heavy to carry it.*  
  ✅ *The box is too heavy to carry.*  
  No repetimos el objeto con `to`.

- ❌ *This is more cheaper.*  
  ✅ *This is much cheaper.*  
  Usamos `much` para intensificar, no `more`.

# Excepciones relevantes

`too` puede ir seguido de `for + persona`:

`The test was too difficult for me.`

`enough` puede ir seguido de `to + infinitivo`:

`She is old enough to travel alone.`

Con `too ... to` y `enough ... to`, el infinitivo no repite el sujeto objeto.

# Mini resumen

- `too + adjetivo` = exceso negativo.
- `adjetivo + enough`; `enough + sustantivo`.
- `much/a lot/far` = gran diferencia; `a bit/slightly` = pequeña diferencia.
