# Resumen

Los cuantificadores expresan cantidad. Elegimos uno u otro según el tipo de
sustantivo (contable o incontable) y el tipo de oración.

`I need some water.`  
`Do you have any questions?`  
`There isn't much time.`

# Objetivos

- Distinguir sustantivos contables e incontables.
- Usar `some` en afirmativas y `any` en negativas y preguntas.
- Usar `much` con incontables y `many` con contables.
- Usar `a lot of` con ambos.
- Diferenciar `a few`/`a little` de `few`/`little`.

# Explicación

## Some y any

`some` se usa en afirmativas:

`I have some money.`

`any` se usa en negativas y preguntas:

`I don't have any money.`  
`Do you have any questions?`

En ofrecimientos y peticiones usamos `some` aunque sea una pregunta:

`Would you like some tea?`

## Much, many y a lot of

`much` con incontables:

`There isn't much milk left.`

`many` con contables:

`There aren't many seats available.`

`a lot of` con ambos:

`We have a lot of work.`  
`There are a lot of people here.`

## A few, a little, few, little

`a few` = algunos (positivo), con contables:

`I have a few ideas.`

`a little` = un poco (positivo), con incontables:

`I have a little time.`

`few` = pocos (negativo), con contables:

`Few people came.`

`little` = poco (negativo), con incontables:

`There is little hope.`

# Forma o estructura

- `some + sustantivo` (afirmativa)
- `any + sustantivo` (negativa/pregunta)
- `much + incontable`; `many + contable`
- `a lot of + contable/incontable`
- `a few / few + contable`; `a little / little + incontable`

# Usos principales

1. **Ofrecer:** `Would you like some coffee?`
2. **Preguntar por existencia:** `Are there any tickets left?`
3. **Cantidad grande:** `There are many reasons to visit.`
4. **Cantidad pequeña positiva:** `I have a few suggestions.`
5. **Cantidad pequeña negativa:** `There is little time to decide.`

# Contrastes importantes

## some frente a any

`I have some apples.` (afirmativa)  
`I don't have any apples.` (negativa)

## much frente a many

`much` con incontables: `much water`.  
`many` con contables: `many bottles`.

## a few frente a few

`a few` = algunos (positivo).  
`few` = casi ninguno (negativo).

# Ejemplos

1. **Could I have some water, please?**
2. **There aren't any chairs left.**
3. **We don't have much time before the train.**
4. **How many people are coming?**
5. **I have a few questions about the plan.**
6. **There is little chance of rain today.**
7. **She has a lot of experience.**
8. **Few students missed the class.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ *I don't have some money.*  
  ✅ *I don't have any money.*  
  Las negativas usan `any`.

- ❌ *There isn't many milk.*  
  ✅ *There isn't much milk.*  
  `milk` es incontable, usa `much`.

- ❌ *How much people are here?*  
  ✅ *How many people are here?*  
  `people` es contable, usa `many`.

- ❌ *I have a little ideas.*  
  ✅ *I have a few ideas.*  
  `ideas` es contable, usa `a few`.

# Excepciones relevantes

`some` se usa en preguntas cuando ofrecemos o pedimos:

`Would you like some tea?`

`any` puede usarse en afirmativas con el sentido de "cualquiera":

`You can take any seat.`

`a lot of` es informal; en registros formales se prefiere `much` o `many`.

# Mini resumen

- `some` afirmativa; `any` negativa/pregunta.
- `much` incontable; `many` contable; `a lot of` ambos.
- `a few/a little` positivo; `few/little` negativo.

# Comprobación rápida autocorregible

1. I don't have ___ money. (negativa)
   - Respuesta: `any`
   - Explicación: las negativas usan `any`.
2. There isn't ___ milk left. (incontable)
   - Respuesta: `much`
   - Explicación: `milk` es incontable.
3. How ___ people are coming? (contable)
   - Respuesta: `many`
   - Explicación: `people` es contable.
4. I have a ___ ideas. (algunas)
   - Respuesta: `few`
   - Explicación: `a few` con contables.
5. Would you like ___ tea? (ofrecimiento)
   - Respuesta: `some`
   - Explicación: los ofrecimientos usan `some`.
