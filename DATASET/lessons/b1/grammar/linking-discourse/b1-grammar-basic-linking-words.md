# Resumen

Los conectores unen ideas y muestran su relación.

`I was tired, so I went to bed early.`  
`I stayed home because it was raining.`  
`Although it was cold, we went for a walk.`

# Objetivos

- Unir ideas con `and`, `but` y `or`.
- Expresar consecuencia con `so`.
- Expresar causa con `because`.
- Expresar contraste con `although`.

# Explicación

## And, but, or

`and` añade información:

`I bought bread and milk.`

`but` contrasta:

`The hotel was nice, but the room was small.`

`or` ofrece opciones:

`Would you like tea or coffee?`

## So y because

`so` introduce la consecuencia:

`It was late, so we took a taxi.`

`because` introduce la causa:

`We took a taxi because it was late.`

## Although

`although` introduce un contraste:

`Although it was expensive, we bought it.`

# Forma o estructura

- `idea + and/but/or + idea`
- `causa + so + consecuencia`
- `consecuencia + because + causa`
- `Although + contraste, + resultado`

# Usos principales

1. **Añadir:** `I like reading and cooking.`
2. **Contrastar:** `The flat is small but cosy.`
3. **Elegir:** `We can walk or take the bus.`
4. **Consecuencia:** `The shop was closed, so we left.`
5. **Causa:** `We left because the shop was closed.`
6. **Contraste:** `Although it rained, we enjoyed the trip.`

# Contrastes importantes

## so frente a because

`so` = consecuencia; `because` = causa.

`It was raining, so we stayed in.`  
`We stayed in because it was raining.`

## but frente a although

`but` une dos cláusulas; `although` introduce la cláusula de contraste.

`It was cold, but we went out.`  
`Although it was cold, we went out.`

# Ejemplos

1. **I wanted to go, but I was too tired.**
2. **She studied hard, so she passed the exam.**
3. **We stayed home because it was snowing.**
4. **Although the film was long, it was interesting.**
5. **You can pay by card or in cash.**
6. **The room was clean and bright.**
7. **He was late because he missed the bus.**
8. **The food was good, but the service was slow.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ *I was tired because I went to bed.*  
  ✅ *I was tired, so I went to bed.*  
  `so` introduce la consecuencia.

- ❌ *Because it was raining, so we stayed in.*  
  ✅ *Because it was raining, we stayed in.*  
  No usamos `so` y `because` juntos.

- ❌ *Although it was cold, but we went out.*  
  ✅ *Although it was cold, we went out.*  
  No combinamos `although` y `but`.

# Excepciones relevantes

`because` puede ir al principio o en medio:

`Because it was late, we left.`  
`We left because it was late.`

`although` puede ir al principio o en medio:

`Although it was late, we stayed.`  
`We stayed although it was late.`

# Mini resumen

- `and` añade; `but` contrasta; `or` elige.
- `so` consecuencia; `because` causa.
- `although` contraste.
- No combinar `because` con `so`, ni `although` con `but`.

# Comprobación rápida autocorregible

1. I was tired, ___ I went to bed. (consecuencia)
   - Respuesta: `so`
   - Explicación: `so` introduce la consecuencia.
2. We stayed home ___ it was raining. (causa)
   - Respuesta: `because`
   - Explicación: `because` introduce la causa.
3. ___ it was cold, we went out. (contraste)
   - Respuesta: `Although`
   - Explicación: `although` introduce el contraste.
4. The room was small ___ cosy. (contraste)
   - Respuesta: `but`
   - Explicación: `but` contrasta dos ideas.
5. Would you like tea ___ coffee? (opción)
   - Respuesta: `or`
   - Explicación: `or` ofrece opciones.
