# Resumen

En una narración combinamos varios tiempos para situar los eventos en el tiempo.

`When we arrived, the band had already started and people were dancing.`

El past simple da los eventos principales, el past continuous describe el fondo,
y el past perfect sitúa eventos anteriores.

# Objetivos

- Usar past simple para eventos principales.
- Usar past continuous para acciones de fondo.
- Usar past perfect para eventos anteriores.
- Ordenar la secuencia con conectores temporales.

# Forma o estructura

- Evento principal: `past simple`
- Fondo: `past continuous`
- Anterior: `past perfect`
- En curso anterior: `past perfect continuous`
- Conectores: `when`, `while`, `by the time`, `as soon as`, `after`, `before`

# Usos principales

1. **Secuencia:** `He locked the door and left.`
2. **Fondo:** `The sun was setting as we arrived.`
3. **Evento anterior:** `She had already eaten when I called.`
4. **Duración anterior:** `They had been waiting for an hour.`
5. **Interrupción:** `I was reading when the phone rang.`

# Explicación

## Past simple

Da los eventos principales de la historia:

`She opened the door and walked in.`

## Past continuous

Describe el fondo o una acción en curso:

`The rain was falling as she walked home.`

## Past perfect

Sitúa un evento anterior a otro:

`By the time we arrived, the film had started.`

## Past perfect continuous

Describe una acción en curso antes de un punto pasado:

`They had been driving for hours when they stopped.`

# Contrastes importantes

## Past simple frente a past continuous

`She cooked dinner.` (evento completo)  
`She was cooking dinner.` (fondo en curso)

## Past simple frente a past perfect

`When I arrived, she left.` (ella se fue después)  
`When I arrived, she had left.` (ella ya se había ido)

# Ejemplos

1. **He locked the door and left.**
2. **The sun was setting as we arrived.**
3. **She had already eaten when I called.**
4. **They had been waiting for an hour.**
5. **I was reading when the phone rang.**
6. **By the time we got there, the show had ended.**
7. **While she was cooking, he set the table.**
8. **After they had finished, they went home.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ *When I arrived, she left.* (si ella ya se había ido)  
  ✅ *When I arrived, she had left.*  
  El evento anterior usa past perfect.

- ❌ *I was reading when the phone was ringing.*  
  ✅ *I was reading when the phone rang.*  
  La interrupción usa past simple.

- ❌ *She had been knowing him for years.*  
  ✅ *She had known him for years.*  
  «know» es un verbo de estado.

# Excepciones relevantes

- Con `while` normalmente usamos past continuous para ambas acciones: `While she was cooking, he was setting the table.`
- Con `by the time` y `as soon as`, el past perfect marca la anterioridad: `By the time we arrived, they had left.`

# Mini resumen

- Past simple = eventos principales.
- Past continuous = fondo.
- Past perfect = eventos anteriores.
- Past perfect continuous = duración anterior.
- Conectores temporales ordenan la secuencia.

# Comprobación rápida autocorregible

1. When I arrived, she ___ (leave, anterior).
   - Respuesta: `had left`
   - Explicación: el evento anterior usa past perfect.
2. I ___ (read) when the phone rang.
   - Respuesta: `was reading`
   - Explicación: el fondo usa past continuous.
3. They ___ (wait) for an hour when the bus came.
   - Respuesta: `had been waiting`
   - Explicación: la duración anterior usa past perfect continuous.
4. She ___ (know) him for years.
   - Respuesta: `had known`
   - Explicación: «know» es un verbo de estado.
5. By the time we arrived, the show ___ (end).
   - Respuesta: `had ended`
   - Explicación: el evento anterior usa past perfect.
