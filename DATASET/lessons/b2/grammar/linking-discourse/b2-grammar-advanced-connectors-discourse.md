# Resumen

Los conectores avanzados muestran cómo se relacionan las ideas: contraste, concesión, causa, consecuencia, adición, condición, ejemplificación y reformulación. En B2 no basta con reconocer el significado aproximado; hay que comprobar qué estructura admite cada conector y si ocupa una posición natural en el texto.

# Objetivos

- Relacionar dos cláusulas con although, whereas, however, nevertheless y therefore.
- Elegir despite/in spite of ante nombre o gerundio y although ante cláusula.
- Usar moreover, in addition, consequently, otherwise y provided that en contextos reales.
- Colocar los marcadores con puntuación y registro adecuados.

# Forma o estructura

- although/even though + sujeto + verbo: Although the data are limited, the trend is clear.
- despite/in spite of + nombre o -ing: Despite the cost, the scheme continued.
- however/nevertheless, + cláusula: The sample was small. Nevertheless, the result was useful.
- therefore/consequently/as a result, + cláusula: The road flooded; therefore, buses were diverted.
- provided that/as long as + cláusula: We can publish it provided that the names are removed.
- otherwise + cláusula: Save a copy; otherwise, the changes may be lost.

# Usos principales

1. **Concesión:** Even though the forecast was uncertain, the team acted.
2. **Contraste paralelo:** Whereas the first model is cheap, the second is more durable.
3. **Consecuencia:** The server failed; consequently, the appointment system stopped.
4. **Adición:** The course is practical; moreover, it includes mentoring.
5. **Condición:** You may quote the findings provided that you cite the source.
6. **Reformulación:** The sample was not representative; in other words, the result needs caution.

# Explicación

Although, even though y whereas introducen una cláusula: Although the route is longer, it is safer. Despite e in spite of introducen un nombre, un pronombre o un gerundio: Despite the delay, we arrived; In spite of arriving late, we joined the meeting. However y therefore son sentence adverbs: suelen ir al principio de una nueva cláusula y se separan con coma, aunque también pueden ocupar otras posiciones.

Los conectores deben reflejar la relación exacta. However y nevertheless presentan contraste; therefore, consequently y as a result presentan consecuencia; moreover e in addition añaden información; otherwise introduce una consecuencia negativa si no se cumple una condición; provided that expresa una condición suficiente.

# Contrastes importantes

## Although frente a despite

Although the room was small introduce una cláusula completa. Despite the small room introduce un nombre. Despite the room being small también es posible porque being small es un gerundio con sujeto.

## However frente a but

But une dos cláusulas dentro de una estructura coordinada. However funciona como marcador discursivo y necesita una puntuación independiente: The plan is expensive. However, it is realistic. No escribas ❌ The plan is expensive, however it is realistic.

## Therefore frente a because

Because introduce la causa; therefore introduce el resultado. The flight was delayed because of fog explica la causa. The flight was delayed; therefore, we missed the connection presenta la consecuencia.

# Ejemplos

1. **Although the evidence is limited, the pattern is consistent.**
2. **Despite the limited evidence, the pattern is worth discussing.**
3. **The figures were incomplete; nevertheless, the committee reached a decision.**
4. **The permit expired; consequently, work stopped.**
5. **The method is inexpensive. Moreover, it is easy to repeat.**
6. **We can extend the trial provided that volunteers agree.**
7. **Back up the files; otherwise, the work may disappear.**

# Errores frecuentes

- ❌ Despite the road was flooded, buses stopped.
- ✅ Although the road was flooded, buses stopped.
- ❌ Although the delay, we continued.
- ✅ Despite the delay, we continued.
- ❌ The plan is costly, however it is realistic.
- ✅ The plan is costly. However, it is realistic.
- ❌ The sample was small, therefore the conclusion is cautious.
- ✅ The sample was small; therefore, the conclusion is cautious.
- ❌ We will publish it providing that names removed.
- ✅ We will publish it provided that the names are removed.

# Excepciones relevantes

- In spite of y despite son muy parecidos, aunque in spite of suele sonar algo más explícito.
- Nevertheless y nonetheless son intercambiables en muchos contextos.
- On the other hand puede introducir una alternativa o un contraste, pero no siempre expresa una consecuencia lógica.
- En textos breves, un conector sencillo puede ser más claro que una cadena de marcadores formales.

# Mini resumen

- Although/even though llevan cláusula; despite/in spite of llevan nombre o gerundio.
- However y nevertheless marcan contraste entre ideas independientes.
- Therefore, consequently y as a result presentan resultados.
- Moreover e in addition añaden información.
- Otherwise y provided that expresan condiciones y consecuencias.

# Comprobación rápida autocorregible

1. ___ the road was flooded, buses continued. (Although)
   - Respuesta: Although
   - Explicación: Although introduce una cláusula completa.
2. ___ the flood, buses continued. (Despite)
   - Respuesta: Despite
   - Explicación: Despite precede a un nombre.
3. The figures were incomplete; ___, we reached a decision. (nevertheless)
   - Respuesta: nevertheless
   - Explicación: Nevertheless marca contraste entre dos ideas.
4. Save a copy; ___, you may lose the changes. (otherwise)
   - Respuesta: otherwise
   - Explicación: Otherwise presenta el resultado de no hacerlo.
5. We can proceed ___ the names are removed. (provided that)
   - Respuesta: provided that
   - Explicación: Provided that introduce una condición suficiente.
